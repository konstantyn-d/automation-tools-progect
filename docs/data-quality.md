# LoopCasts data quality report

Generated by `scripts/clean_data.py` from the Dropbox export. Aggregates only, no personal data.

## Catalogue

- 60 courses. Level written 9 different ways (Advanced, Beginner, advanced, beginner, easy, expert, intermediate, medium, nan); mapped to beginner / intermediate / advanced, 4 without level.
- Duration in 10 formats (99, 99 min, 999, 999 min, 99min, 9:99:99, ...); stored as minutes.
- 11 courses without a single price (prices used: [39.0, 49.0]).

## Vimeo

- 599 videos, 7278 minutes. Public (privacy=anybody): 3. Without chapters: 236. Without description: 135. With captions: 0.
- Catalogue video count differs from Vimeo for 4 courses, duration (>5 min) for 23. The database uses the Vimeo numbers.
- Courses in the catalogue without videos in Vimeo: 1 (n8n basics (2026 edition)).

## Orders

- 1007 orders (2025 + 2026). Country written 37 different ways; normalised to ISO codes. Dates in 28 formats.
- 42 EU company orders without VAT number; 21 single-course orders where the course cannot be identified from the remark; 3 invalid e-mail addresses.
- Price column written in 8 formats (e.g. 149, 149,00, € 149).
- 10 orders of 2026 have no invoice number (never invoiced or not written down).

## Invoices

- 282 invoices in the main register (2026-0001..2026-0283). Duplicate numbers: 2026-0029, 2026-0071. Missing numbers: 2026-0023, 2026-0088, 2026-0241.
- 3 invoices where excl. + VAT ≠ total. 9 invoices where the VAT does not match the VAT rule for that customer (2026-0015, 2026-0021, 2026-0044, 2026-0071, 2026-0166, 2026-0244, 2026-0247, 2026-0255 …).
- 1 invoices cannot be linked to an order/customer (register has only a name, no e-mail; link goes through the invoice number in the orders sheet).
- Paid status only exists as a cell colour: main register 206 green, 6 orange, 69 uncoloured; monthly register 1105 green, 167 uncoloured. Open according to colours: €15,121.68.
- Monthly invoices use a second numbering series (M26-01-001 …) in a separate tab.

## Bank

- 1486 lines, 1450 incoming. With a recognisable invoice number (main or monthly series) in the message: 286 (20%). Empty message: 225. Most common amounts: €18.15 ×1183, €180.29 ×121, €15.0 ×70.

## Members

- 1961 rows in the old users table, 1940 unique e-mails (21 duplicate rows, merged: one login per e-mail, every row kept as an entitlement).
- 123 accounts still active although the end date has passed (on 2026-06-30). In the new model access is computed from the end date, so this cannot happen again.
- Passwords are MD5 hashes of 'first name + number'. They are NOT imported; every account gets a 'set your password' link (bcrypt from then on).
- 18 single-course accounts where the old table does not say which course (not in the 2025/2026 orders either).

## Dates

- 30 dates were typed month first (e.g. 10/13/2023); detected because the 'month' was above 12. Ambiguous ones (both parts ≤ 12) are read day first, as in Belgium.
- 36 order dates were written without a year in Dutch or English (e.g. 13 jan); the year was taken from the sheet (orders 2025 / 2026).
- 9 dates could not be read at all (13 mrt, 19 jun, 24 apr, 24 okt, 27 mrt, 5 jun); stored as empty.
